Our Story

In 2019, Micko Dedios, together with his friends Kyle Lising, Oliver Sazon, and Christian Miranda, turned their shared passion for baking into AC Bakehouse — a cozy spot where people could enjoy freshly made pastries, breads, and desserts crafted with care and the finest ingredients.

What started as a small local bakery has quickly become a beloved destination in Angeles City, known for its hand-crafted treats and warm, welcoming atmosphere. Every item is made fresh daily, staying true to the philosophy of quality, flavor, and love in every bite.

Their signature Passionfruit Donut, made from locally sourced ingredients, embodies this philosophy: transforming simple, quality ingredients into unforgettable flavors.

Our Process

Before the sun rises, the bakers begin creating the day's offerings.

Nothing is pre-made or frozen. Every donut is shaped, fried, and frosted by hand. Every croissant is layered with real butter for perfect flakiness. Every loaf of bread is allowed to ferment naturally to develop rich, authentic flavors.

Visitors can watch through our kitchen window as flour, butter, and passion come together to create treats that have delighted the community for over 5 years.
